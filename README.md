# Make the Judge Smile

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d`
("Make the Judge Smile", $5 pool, top 3 places).

`main.ts` writes one line of at most 200 characters to `/output/entry.txt`:
a small good thing that happened today, written to win a pairwise judge
that weighs, in order: on-subject, warmth, surprise, one concrete detail,
fresh wording.

The line: a toddler on the morning train offers the narrator half her
biscuit, solemn as a diplomat signing a treaty. Warmth (a child sharing),
surprise (the diplomat simile), one concrete detail (the morning train,
half a biscuit), fresh wording, 99 characters.

No network, no randomness, no dependencies. Runs under plain Node 22.
